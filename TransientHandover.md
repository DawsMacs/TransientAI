**The baby** (`baby school` / `baby ask` / `baby learn`) is one system:

1. Taught **units of lessons** (the teacher groups them, never says how to learn them), or finds the units itself (`baby discover`; fails on real text, see section 10).
2. For each unit it chooses how to learn it **from facts it holds back**: *remember* (voting memory), *rule* (robust ridge fit), *select* (bigger/smaller), *program* (searched expression, learned library, competing explanations; and now **partial programs** that fit most lessons and only ever guess), *network* (rarely).
3. **Questions** go through: unreadable numbers refused -> a word never met refused (except for a partial-program unit) -> routed by a sparse incremental table with a **recognition floor that follows the margins as it learns** -> the unit's mechanism answers.
4. **Careful by default** (Daws): it states as fact only what lies inside the range taught *or checked* (the **reach**: lessons that ask for the *same function* must cover every number of the question, a staircase not a box). Beyond that it offers a **labeled guess** with a stated hit rate (never an `answer`), or nothing.
5. **It asks and checks itself** (`wonder`, `check_myself`: edges, corner, and the frontier of what can be asked) and learns the answers; a simulated teacher makes ~5-7 questions per numeric unit enough to turn every refusal the reach rule causes into a stated fact.
6. **Skeptical of its teacher** (on by default since 2026-10-22): an answer to one of its own checks that contradicts a settled rule is **held**, not learned, until repeated (`Learned.disputed`; held answers are saved in the school file). An established exact program survives up to 3 contradicting lessons.
7. It estimates its own noise and sets its own reliability bar; keeps learning after school (surprise gate, pen, `reorganize`/`sleep`, `--compact`).

## 6. Guards that must stay (each one was learned from a failure; do not "simplify" them away)

- **Behavioral uniqueness / believability:** a built program is trusted only where its smallest fits agree (`alts`), and only if the lessons carry more bits than the search spent. A *partial* program must also pay for naming each exception (MDL), and **answers only as a labeled guess, never a fact; it is never library vocabulary; it counts as an open unit** (revisited when the library grows: `capsum` regression).
- **Reliability bar** adaptive, floor 0.8, 0.9 when clean. A mechanism below it must not answer.
- **Memory:** votes among matching lessons (a tie refuses), content words compared as a sequence with repeats, number words never glue; lesson identity = words in order.
- **Library:** only *settled* programs are vocabulary; a unit is never explained by its own contributions; a unit contributes a behavior once.
- **Cross-validation:** facts that share a bag of words in one fold; merge parts stratified.
- **Unreadable numbers** refused at `ask`, at the CLI entry and when teaching; **never ask for an answer it cannot read back**.
- **Reach is a staircase over lessons that ask for the SAME FUNCTION** (rule: total word-weight vector; selection: sign; program: all). **Never define "which lessons count" by a glue heuristic** (it moves as facts are added, so teaching more reduced coverage) and never by the rule's `all_ops`/`active_ops` words (template words like "what"/"is" carry weight).
- **A box of per-operand ranges vouches for unchecked corners** (two confirmed edges covered the corner between them: 4 wrong facts): keep the staircase.
- **A guess is never an `answer`**; every harness counts right/wrong on facts alone; guesses are scored apart with a stated, calibrated rate.
- **A lesson that contradicts a rule outside what the rule explains refutes it there** (no guess beyond its explained reach); what it was told wins over a refuted rule.
- **A repaired program never lets its repair lessons vouch for their neighbors** (one point fits many laws: a cap at 24 explains 24+12=24 as well as a reflection at 30), **and must agree with the old rule everywhere inside the range the other lessons cover** (a coincidental program once rewrote a selection unit and stated 240 wrong facts). **After ANY change to repair, re-run `compare --teacher 200 --wrong 0.2 --persistent`: it must read ~87.4 % right, ~39 wrong facts all repeats, 0 elsewhere on seed 1, and 0 elsewhere on seeds 2 and 3 too** (it read 88.0 % / 28 before the repair step and 87.97 % / 25 before `refuted` used the staircase; the 5 % persistent run reads 99.11 % / 4) (there is no unit test for the inside-range guard). The reach functions' `skip` lists must be sorted (`binary_search`).
- **A rebuilt law does not overrule the one it replaced until `RETIRE_AFTER` (2) lessons told after the repair have separated them** (`School::repair_veto`; `ProgramRule.before` is saved, the old law is re-derived on demand): a fact the new law states that the old law contradicts is a guess (a refusal on a refuted unit or where the old law is silent). Without it one wrong lesson beyond the taught range, rebuilt into a law, made 68-100 wrong facts "elsewhere" under a persistent teacher on seeds 2 and 3.
- **A repaired program that still explains every lesson is never swapped for a fresh fit** (`refit_in_place`, `relearn_unit`): the fresh fit does not know the repair's numbers and the smallest program that fits can be a coincidence (`second + (first min 18)` vs `min(first+second, 30)`) whose lessons then vouch for what lies below them. Run the careful battery at `--limit 1` AND `--limit 4` after any change to repair, refit or reach.
- **Skeptical by default** for the baby's own checks; **callers of `learn_wondered` must handle `Learned.disputed`** (ask again), or use `learn_wondered_sure` when modeling a teacher who repeats themselves.
- **The recognition floor follows the margins** (stored ratio to the leave-one-out margin), never above its teaching-time value.
- **The energy meter:** the idle baseline is re-measured **before every measurement**, the lower of before and after is used, a non-positive reading is "n/a" (never 0.00 J), no CPU-share scaling, a warning when other processes use >25 % of the busy CPU time. Quiet machine required for clean figures.
- **Report WRONG apart from refused, and deceptive kinds apart from the 0-wrong headline.** Add must-refuse questions with every capability (the same ambiguity family bit **four** times).
- **Wrong facts are accounted by fact (unit + numbers, any phrasing)**, not by exact question text, when deciding "repeat of what it was told" versus "damage elsewhere".
- **A fresh exact fit that explains a few contradicting lessons is a repair, not an upgrade** (`School::rebuild_policy`, in `relearn_unit` and `refit_in_place`): the contradicting lessons are marked unproven, the replaced law stays a competing explanation (`repair_veto`), and a program adopted this way (`rebuilt`) is re-examined when the library grows. Refusing the rebuild outright breaks the case where the contradiction is the truth. A wrong lesson can pose as support for a rival law (a tens digit rebuilt as `b apart ((a + b) mod 10)`).
- **The two cost shortcuts rest on arguments, not on luck:** `settled_program_explaining_all` (only an exact program with no rivals that still says every lesson, the new one included: a smaller program fitting all lessons would have fitted the earlier ones) and `just_relearned` (only when `learn` has just re-decided the unit and left its state as it was, from the same lessons and library). Do not extend either to rules or selections (a ridge fit drifts with every lesson), and do not add a back-off for memorize-only units: it was byte-identical on every clean stream and the whole battery and still changed two answers (one new wrong fact) on the 20 % wrong-teacher stream.
- **Byte-compare answers, on a wrong-teacher stream too, before accepting a change that is meant to be answer-neutral** (`--out` files of the old and new binary, or the goldens plus an old commit built in a `git worktree`).
- **The goldens pin what the baby answers on the reference platform.** A change that is meant to change answers runs `golden/check.sh update` in the same commit and says why; otherwise a difference is a regression (CI enforces it on the reference row once PR #4 is merged). The goldens cover one clean adaptive stream and the static exam, not the wrong-teacher streams.
- **Tests never use a real `Meter`** (`Meter::off()`), and every assertion about the platform states the property meant (a figure is positive or NaN, never zero), not an inference from a path existing.

## 9. Doubts and honest caveats (state these when reporting; they are not boilerplate)

- **Almost everything is synthetic and tidy**, in number words, in rule-shaped units: the baby's home ground. "0 % wrong" is a spot check plus must-refuse batteries, not a proof; **every harder test found a new hole** (regime changes, corners, glue, stale meters, wrong teachers). Expect more.
- **The simulated teachers**: errors are uniform over other number words, independent of difficulty; a real person repeats themselves more (the "persistent" column bounds that); one world and one seed for most noisy-teacher figures.
- **The language is mine**: mechanisms, operations, thresholds (see the list in section 8). The baby builds and selects inside them.
- **Energy figures are measurements to within about +-30 %** on a busy desktop (see the meter guards), and my own *estimates* were far off. Transformer comparisons use a plain-Rust transformer, no real sweep except 24 PyTorch settings.
- **The transformer overtakes the baby on raw accuracy for unseen questions from 60 units** (100 % vs ~90 % at 1,000 units) because careful mode makes the baby refuse ~9 % there; the baby's advantage is honesty, extrapolation, no forgetting, and teaching cost, **not** raw unseen accuracy.
- **Real text is mostly unsolved**: SVAMP is adversarially built; routing by words caps near 60 %; nothing shows behavior on millions of real sentences or at "frontier" quality. Treat that as the open frontier, not a settled conclusion either way.
- **The exam is tidy and static**: synthetic number-word worlds, a simulated always-right teacher, no real text (licenses forbid redistributing it), joules for the processor package only. The baby's trap-honesty depends on the teacher's grouping of lessons and on calibration material; a successor should say what it needs.
- **Real-text figures are fragile**: SVAMP guesses-right ranges 35-73 % across six splits, one corpus is adversarial, the other related to it; ~25 % of questions still get a wrong labeled guess.
- **The veto trades coverage for honesty** under a persistently wrong teacher (see START HERE item 4); **adapting is still ~600x the cost of answering** (36 mJ per told answer vs 59 uJ per question, from ~5,800x at the start of 2026-10-23); the energy figures are upper bounds on a desktop (+-30 %, the meter warns about other processes).
- **Every explanation I wrote down unmeasured today was wrong at least once** (the program search was the cost; a guess bar; a work budget; a duplicate count). Trust the stage timers and the traces, not the story.
- **Platform claims are "CI-tested", nothing more:** the runners are VMs, one image per row; the goldens cover the static exam and one clean adaptive stream (not the wrong-teacher streams); the fingerprint covers stated input ranges only; the math differences found are one last-bit step, which does not prove that no input anywhere can flip a near-tie; the hash-order hazard is unfixed, so a golden mismatch elsewhere could be a false alarm (hence three runs per machine); joules are Linux with a readable RAPL counter only.
- **The transformer comparison is one small, untuned classifier** over answer words (so it cannot produce a number it was never taught), on a busy machine (joules are upper bounds); a larger or tuned model, a model that generates numbers, a wrong-teacher stream for it, and the pretrained-model comparison (needs the GPU) are not done.
